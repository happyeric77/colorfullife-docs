## What it is

Home Assistant runs the smart home: lights, sensors, presence and the
ESPHome-based devices around the house.

## Story

Most of the work here is reliability, not features: BLE proxies that recover
themselves, Zigbee networks that survive a reboot, and automations that fail
quietly instead of loudly.
