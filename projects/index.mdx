Every project I build or maintain. Projects are entities: they have a
lifecycle, a story, and can produce journal entries over time.

Projects can have **subprojects** (`parent` in front matter). Services that
only make sense inside a project stay components — Grafana is a component of
the K3s Cluster, not a project of its own, until it grows a story and lifecycle
worth tracking.
