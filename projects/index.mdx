Selected systems, products and infrastructure that are worth understanding as
long-lived work. A Project is not everything I touch — it is durable work that
deserves its own canonical page: a story, a lifecycle, and possibly several
Journal entries over time.

Some projects have a **parent project** (`parentProject` in front matter), but
that hierarchy is a structural hint, not the main structure of the site.
Services and capabilities that only make sense inside a project stay
components or sections of its page — Grafana is a component of the Kubernetes
cluster, not a project of its own.
