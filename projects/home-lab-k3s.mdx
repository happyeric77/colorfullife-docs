## What it is

A single-node (for now) K3s cluster running on Proxmox, hosting everything
from Tailscale routing to monitoring. This is a subproject of
[Home Lab](/projects/home-lab).

## Components

Components live here, not as projects of their own:

- **Argo CD** — GitOps delivery for cluster workloads.
- **Grafana / Prometheus** — metrics and dashboards.
- **Tailscale operator** — cluster ingress into the home network.

## Story

The cluster replaced a collection of docker-compose stacks that had become
impossible to reason about. Moving it to GitOps was the turning point: the
cluster became reproducible instead of remembered.
