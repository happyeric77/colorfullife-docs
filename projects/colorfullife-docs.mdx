## What it is

ColorfulLife Docs is the site you are reading. It started as a pile of
technical notes and is being rebuilt as an **engineering journal**: the
stories from real work, the Projects that give them durable context, and the
technologies that connect them.

## Structure

The site has four content systems:

- **Projects** — curated canonical hubs for durable work, like this one.
- **Journal** — the primary publishing stream: dated entries produced by the work.
- **Topics** — cross-project discovery paths (Docusaurus, Kubernetes, GitOps).
- **Archive** — the legacy technical notes, kept for reference.

## Story

The original site was documentation-only: every note was a page in one big
docs tree. That worked for reference, but it had no place for the actual
work — the decisions, dead ends and retrospectives. The rebuild introduces a
Journal as the primary publishing stream and Projects as durable context,
without deleting or rewriting the archive.
