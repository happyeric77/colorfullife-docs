## What it is

The Home Lab is the umbrella project for everything running on hardware at
home: virtualization, DNS, VPN access, home automation, monitoring and the
Kubernetes cluster that hosts most services.

## Subprojects

- [K3s Cluster](/projects/home-lab-k3s) — the Kubernetes cluster behind most
  self-hosted services.
- [Home Assistant](/projects/home-assistant) — home automation and the
  devices around it.

Everything else inside these subprojects — Grafana, Prometheus, Pi-hole and
friends — is a component: real, documented, but not a project with its own
lifecycle yet.

## Story

The lab started as one machine running a handful of services and grew into
something that needed structure: a cluster, GitOps, monitoring and a VPN that
does not fall over. Most of the technical notes in the
[Archive](/archive) came out of this project.
